# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Duncan Bartley, an experienced data professional evaluating his next role.
- A trusted job-search agent or helper who will refine the resume, qualify roles, and prepare applications with Duncan's approval.

## Product Purpose

Help Duncan tighten a two-page resume into credible one-page alternatives, compare positioning choices, answer the questions that materially change a search, and triage a ranked set of relevant remote-US or Washington, DC-area roles.

Success means Duncan can select a resume direction, supply missing search constraints, and give an agent a compact evidence packet for thoughtful applications.

## Positioning

The project connects resume editing and role discovery in one static, portable case file: every resume direction is derived from Duncan's actual experience, while every role is presented with fit evidence, gaps, and a direct application link.

## Operating Context

- Source resume: `source/original-resume.pdf`.
- Job discovery: authenticated TrueUp searches covering data analysis, data engineering, governance, analytics engineering, business intelligence, data quality, integration, operations, reporting, SQL, Salesforce data, and stewardship.
- Distribution: a no-build static site suitable for GitHub Pages.
- Use: compare resume variants, expand a full-page preview, answer intake questions, filter roles, and hand the result to a trusted application helper.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve Duncan's real name, employment history, education, tools, domains, and claims.
- Anonymize phone, email, and LinkedIn/contact destinations in public-facing output.
- Do not invent accomplishments, metrics, compensation goals, work authorization, clearance, or preferences.
- Resume variants must be single-page, vertically tight, and favor single-line bullets.
- The role set is a first-pass shortlist, not a final application decision; availability and requirements must be rechecked at the source.
- The original PDF stays private and is excluded from version control.
- Open decisions: target compensation, preferred titles, seniority ceiling, industry exclusions, commute radius, travel, clearance, sponsorship, and application authority.

## Evidence on Hand

- Duncan's two-page professional resume with experience at Callahan & Associates and Thomson Reuters Court Express.
- TrueUp result-card metadata collected on July 27, 2026, including role, employer, location, listing age, salary when shown, tools, company signals, and application links.
- No editable resume source file, verified work authorization details, compensation target, or final role preferences were provided.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve truth while tightening language.
2. Show alternatives side by side before choosing a canonical resume.
3. Expose uncertainty and gaps instead of manufacturing confidence.
4. Optimize for a human agent's scanning and decision workflow.
5. Keep the artifact portable, private-source-safe, and easy to host.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, responsive reading, and printable one-page resumes.
